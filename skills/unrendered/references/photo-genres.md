# Photo genre recipes — cast the photographer

Pick the genre whose *real-world photographer* would plausibly take this picture. Copy the capture block into the brief and adapt. Aspect ratio suggestions use model-native sizes from `models.json`.

Format of each recipe: **Who/why · Gear · Light · Look & artifacts · Framing · Avoid**

---

### 1. Phone snapshot (vernacular) — maximum believability
- **Who/why:** a friend/family member capturing a moment to share in a group chat.
- **Gear:** recent smartphone main camera, ~26mm equiv., f/1.8, auto HDR.
- **Light:** whatever is there — ceiling lights, window, streetlight.
- **Look:** deep depth of field (everything roughly sharp), computational HDR (lifted shadows, slightly flat), mild oversharpening, slight noise in shadows indoors, JPEG.
- **Framing:** 4:3 or 3:4, handheld, horizon 1–3° off, subject slightly off-centre, a bit of headroom wasted, edge clutter.
- **Avoid:** creamy bokeh, studio light, perfect composition.

### 2. On-camera flash snapshot (party, night out, 2000s/disposable vibe)
- **Gear:** compact or disposable film camera / phone flash.
- **Light:** direct flash: bright faces, hard shadow on wall behind, fast falloff to dark background, specular foreheads.
- **Look:** slight overexposure of nearest subject, possibly red-eye-free, warm ambient barely visible, film grain or phone noise.
- **Framing:** spontaneous, cut-off limbs, people mid-laugh.

### 3. Street / documentary
- **Who:** street photographer on foot.
- **Gear:** 35mm prime on a rangefinder or mirrorless, f/5.6–8, 1/250s, ISO 400–1600 (zone focusing).
- **Light:** available — hard sun with deep shadows, or overcast; neon at night.
- **Look:** fine-to-moderate grain, slight motion in passers-by, honest colour or B/W (Tri-X look).
- **Framing:** 3:2, layered foreground/background, decisive moment, subjects unaware of camera.
- **Avoid:** posed eye contact, empty streets "for clarity", glossy grade.

### 4. Photojournalism / news-style (fictional scenes only — never fake real events)
- **Gear:** full-frame DSLR, 24–70mm f/2.8, high ISO in bad light.
- **Look:** functional exposure, noise, motion blur, imperfect focus on secondary subjects.
- **Framing:** 3:2, tight, reactive.

### 5. Environmental portrait (editorial)
- **Who:** magazine photographer portraying a person in their workplace/home.
- **Gear:** 35mm or 50mm, f/2.8–4, ISO 400–800.
- **Light:** window light as key + practicals; maybe one bounced flash.
- **Look:** retained skin texture, natural colour, background readable but softer.
- **Framing:** 3:2 or 4:5, subject 1/3 of frame, real tools/objects of their life around.

### 6. Classic headshot / portrait (pro, still real)
- **Gear:** 85mm f/1.8 at f/2.2–2.8, ISO 100–200.
- **Light:** large softbox or window at 45°, reflector fill, catchlights matching the window shape.
- **Look:** sharp eyes, falling-off ears, texture-preserving retouch only (no smoothing), true skin colour.
- **Framing:** 4:5 or 2:3, eye level, slight head tilt, genuine micro-expression.

### 7. Wedding / event guest or pro
- **Gear:** 24–70mm / 70–200mm, f/2.8, high ISO indoors, bounced flash.
- **Look:** warm tungsten venue, mixed light, motion in dancing, some faces missed focus.

### 8. Product — real commercial studio
- **Gear:** 100mm macro, f/8–11, ISO 100, tripod.
- **Light:** describable setup: e.g. large strip softbox camera-right, white V-flat camera-left, gradient from a gridded backlight.
- **Look:** real material behaviour (micro-scratches on aluminium, fingerprints removed but dust specks possible, true label printing), physically correct reflections and contact shadow.
- **Framing:** 1:1 or 4:5; product 60–70% of frame.
- **Avoid:** floating objects, impossible reflections, splashes frozen like CGI unless described with high-speed flash details.

### 9. Product — lifestyle / on location
- Product in use in a real home; window light; human hands with real skin; brand-agnostic clutter; slight DOF.

### 10. Food
- **Gear:** 50–100mm, f/2.8–4, side window light from behind (backlight 10–11 o'clock).
- **Look:** crumbs, drips, steam only if hot and backlit, uneven browning, real garnish, used cutlery.
- **Avoid:** perfect symmetry, glossy plastic sheen.

### 11. Real-estate / interior
- **Gear:** 16–20mm, tripod at chest height, verticals corrected, f/8.
- **Look:** flash-ambient blend or HDR, windows readable, warm lamps on, real but tidy clutter.

### 12. Landscape / travel
- **Gear:** 24–70mm, f/8–11, ISO 100, or phone wide for travel snaps.
- **Light:** specific time and weather; haze with distance (aerial perspective), real cloud structure.
- **Avoid:** over-saturated sky, impossible sun positions, too many "epic" elements in one frame.

### 13. Sports / action
- **Gear:** 400mm f/2.8, 1/2000s, ISO 1600–3200, from sideline.
- **Look:** compressed background crowd as blur, sweat, dirt on kit, frozen motion with slight blur on extremities.

### 14. Night / low light ambient
- **Gear:** 35mm f/1.4 at ISO 6400, 1/60s — or phone night mode.
- **Look:** visible noise, mixed sodium/LED colours, blown light sources with flare/halation, crushed shadows.

### 15. Film emulation (when a "vintage/analog" vibe is wanted)
- Portra 400: warm skin, pastel highlights, fine grain, slight overexposure. Ektar 100: saturated, punchy. Tri-X 400: B/W, contrasty grain. Cinestill 800T: tungsten balance, red halation around lights. Disposable: flash falloff, soft corners, vignetting.
- Say "scanned 35mm film negative" and name 1–2 artifacts (dust specks, slight light leak) — never all.

### 16. Selfie
- **Gear:** front phone camera ~22–24mm equiv., arm's length (~50 cm) → mild nose enlargement, arm visible at edge, deep DOF, beauty filter OFF.

### 17. Corporate / team / office
- Real office (not a stock glass-cube office): cables, post-its, mismatched mugs, monitors with blurred content; window + overhead fluorescent mix; people mid-conversation, not all looking at camera.

### 18. Drone / aerial
- 24mm equiv. on drone, top-down or 30–45° oblique, haze, real road markings, shadows consistent with time of day, slight lens distortion.

### 19. Macro / nature close-up
- 100mm macro, f/8–f/16, flash or diffused sun, thin DOF even at f/11, dust/pollen, imperfect leaf edges, insect anatomy correct (6 legs, correct antenna count).

### 20. Archival / historical (fictional scenes)
- Period-correct process: daguerreotype, glass plate, Kodachrome, 1970s instamatic. Specify era-appropriate clothing/objects only; aging artifacts (scratches, fading) subtle.
