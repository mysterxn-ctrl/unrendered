Plan

The model comes from blind-preference leaderboards. Unrendered switches only on a clear lead: larger than both 95% confidence intervals added together, or at least 25 Elo and confirmed by a second leaderboard. A tie keeps the current model. Default: GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst.

Brief

“A man drinking coffee.”

One line in. The skill answers the questions a photographer would ask before lifting the camera.

Photographer
Candid documentary photo, taken by a friend across the table.
Scene
Small neighbourhood café, grey November morning, rain on the window, half a croissant on scratched wood.
Subject
Man in his late 40s, salt-and-pepper beard, pilled navy sweater.
Pose
Seated alone, right elbow on the table, cup by the handle just below his mouth, left hand flat beside the saucer.
Expression
Lips pursed to blow on the coffee, eyes half-lowered to the window, brows relaxed.
Camera
50 mm · f/2.8 · 1/125 s · ISO 1600 · focus on the near eye
Light
Overcast window light from camera left, about 6500 K. Faint warm pendant behind.
Texture
Visible pores, stray beard hairs, pilled wool, worn wood grain, slight ISO grain.
Avoid
Airbrushed skin, stiff posed stance, disembodied hands, glossy commercial look, cinematic grade.

Inspect

  • Wrong or duplicated limbs and body parts.
  • Unrealistic body positions.
  • Smooth, AI-sloppy surfaces: skin, paper and stone look polished.
  • One cinematic grade on every surface: teal shadows, gold highlights.
  • The checklist has no words, only grey bars.
  • The tablet shows a chart with nothing to read.
  • A desk without a single trace of use.

Reads as a render. Repair: rebrief with every hand assigned, regenerate, check limbs, pose, texture and text again.
