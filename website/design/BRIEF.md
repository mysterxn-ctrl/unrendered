## Honesty constraints

- Live surface means real markup running real logic. Model selection runs the skill's actual decision rule on the real Artificial Analysis rows bundled in the repo. The brief compiler, the rubric weights and the finishing math are the skill's own, computed in the page.
- Anything that is sample data says so on its face ("sample review of the brand render").
- No invented statistics. Only Elo numbers from the bundled snapshots and the finishing parameters themselves are shown as numbers.
- No time references in copy (user rule): no "today", no snapshot dates.

## Feeling curve (written before the acts)

```
1  Recognition   "this is a tool, not an ad": a session window already holding a request
2  Trust         the model is chosen by arithmetic you can read, not by taste
3  Recognition+  one plain sentence grows into the thoughts of a photographer
4  Unease        the lens finds what is wrong with a perfect render: no grain, no falloff, too clean
5  Awe (PEAK)    the render develops in front of you: grain, falloff, fringing, softer highlights
6  Agency        your own sentence, a cursor, and a brief that answers back
```

Adjacent acts differ: tool, trust, recognition, unease, awe, agency.

## The peak

Visitor sentence: **"I scrolled and the clean CGI render slowly turned into something a lens had seen, with the numbers counting up beside it."** Lives in act 5. It gets the largest span on the page and the only full-width image. Act 4 is the quiet before it: small, inspective, mostly text.

## Tell-someone sentence

**It's the site where a render develops into a photograph while you scroll, and then it writes the photographer's brief for whatever you type.**
